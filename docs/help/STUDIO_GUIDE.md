# Studio guide

A plain-English guide to the app for studio staff. The in-app assistant reads this guide, so keep it accurate when screens change.

## Who can do what

| | Owner / Admin | Front of House | Instructor |
|---|---|---|---|
| See the schedule | Everything | Everything | Their own classes |
| Class lists (who's booked) | Every class | Every class | Their own classes |
| Book, cancel, move or refund for a client | Yes | No | No |
| Take attendance | Any class (and correct it after it locks) | Any class, until it locks | Their own classes, from an hour before until it locks |
| Create, change or cancel classes | Yes | No | No |
| Client profiles, history, packages and fees | Yes | Contact details on class lists only | Contact details for their own classes |
| Sell and manage packages | Yes | No | No |
| Cover | Choose who covers, approve, assign | No | Ask for cover on their own classes |
| Messages (Communication) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Reports, marketing, payroll, staff, studio rules | Yes | No | Their own pay (if the studio allows) |
| Agents and the assistant's settings | Yes | No | No |

Owners can do everything admins can. Only the owner can add or remove admins.

## Finding your way around

The menu on the left is grouped by what you're doing. Staff see:

- **Day to day:** Home (or My day for instructors), Schedule (or My schedule), Cover requests, Communication, Announcements, Pickups (Front of House: shop orders and rewards to hand over).
- **Clients & sales:** Clients, Leads, Sales, Packages, Shop, Fees, Feedback.
- **Growth:** Marketing, Reports, Reviews.
- **Team:** Staff & pay, Payroll, Agents.
- **Setup:** Class types, Templates, Locations & rooms, Closures & holidays, Studio rules.
- **Account:** My pay and My reviews (instructors), Preferences.

Clients see **Classes** (Book a class, My bookings), **Packages & rewards** (My packages, Rewards, Refer a friend, and Shop when there's something in it) and **Account** (Feedback, Injuries & conditions, Profile & privacy, Preferences).

The **Ask** button (bottom right) opens the assistant. The **bell** at the top shows messages from your studio's agents.

## Home

Home is the first page staff see. Instructors see it as **My day**, about their own classes. Clients don't have it; their first page is Book a class.

- **Today:** how many people are booked into today's classes, how full the day is, and the class that's on now or up next (tap **Open class**).
- **Today's classes:** every class today, with what needs doing: no instructor, a waiver missing, attendance to take. Tap a class to open it.
- **Needs attention:** a short to-do list, most urgent first. Each item says **Urgent**, **Soon** or **When you can**, and has a button that takes you where to sort it (cover requests, fees, packages, pickups, messages). You only see things you can act on: Front of House never sees fees or pay, and instructors see their own classes and cover requests sent to them. When there's nothing, it says **All caught up**.
- **How it's going:** bookings, how full classes were, new clients and (owners and admins) money in, for the last 7 or 30 days, each against the period before, with a small daily trend. These are the same numbers as Reports.
- **The week ahead:** how full each of the next 7 days is so far. Rest the pointer on a day (or tap it) for the details, or choose **Show as table**. Tapping a day opens it on the schedule.
- **Coming up:** closures in the next 30 days, and quiet classes in the next 3 days that might be worth a nudge to clients.
- **Location:** choose one location at the top to see just that location. New clients and money in are always for the whole studio, and say so.

## The schedule

- **Templates** (Setup, Templates) are saved class details. To delete one, choose **Delete** on its row (or in the template). You're asked first. Classes and repeating schedules made from it stay exactly as they are; the template just can't be used for new classes.
- **Add a class:** Schedule, then **+ New class**. Start from a template to fill most details in. Choose "Repeats" to make it recurring. Shortcut: double-click an empty time on the calendar (owners and admins, on a computer). You're asked first, so a stray double-click doesn't start anything.
- **Move a class to another time:** drag it to the new time or day on the calendar. You're asked to confirm before anything changes, and told about any clashes. Everyone booked or waiting is told about the new time. For a recurring class, only that one moves.
- **No room?** If a location is one open space, choose **No room** in the room list. The class shows at the location, with no room clashes to worry about.
- **Add an appointment** (a private session, not on the public schedule): **+ New appointment**. You can add the client straight away. You'll be warned if you leave the client empty, because appointments are usually only booked once confirmed.
- **Change one class:** tap it, then **Edit**. For recurring classes you choose "this class", "this and later" or "the whole series".
- **Change or cancel many classes at once:** **Edit many**. Either pick classes on the calendar, or choose them with filters (dates up to a year ahead, class types, instructors, location, days of the week and start time). Then change anything you could set when creating a class, or cancel them all.
- **Cancel a class:** tap it, then **Cancel class**. Everyone booked is told and gets their class back.
- **See cancelled classes:** tick **Show cancelled** next to the filters on the Schedule (staff only; clients never see cancelled classes). They show struck through. Open one to see why and when it was cancelled, who cancelled it, and everyone who was booked. Cancelled classes can't be brought back; add a new class at the same time instead.
- **Who cancelled a class:** open the class and choose the **Cancellations** tab (next to **Class list**). It lists everyone whose booking was cancelled: when, how long before class, whether they did it themselves or the studio did (with the reason), whether they got the class back, and any late fee. People moved to another class are listed too. Owners, admins, Front of House and the class's own instructor can see it.
- **Auto-cancel:** a class can cancel itself if fewer than a set number of people are booked a set number of hours before. Set it on a template or on the class.
- **ClassPass:** in the class's settings, switch on ClassPass bookings and choose how many spots ClassPass can have. Unused ClassPass spots stay open to members.

## Closures and holidays

Owners and admins add days the studio is closed under **Setup**, **Closures & holidays**.

- **Add a closure:** **+ Add closure**. Give it a name clients will see (like "Hari Raya"), choose one location or all of them, the first and last days you're closed, and an optional message ("Back on Monday!"). The window shows what will happen before you save: how many classes are on those days and how many people are booked.
- **Classes already on those days** are cancelled in one go, after you confirm. Everyone booked or on the waitlist gets **one** message listing all their cancelled classes, not one per class, and instructors are told about the classes they were teaching. You choose the refund (class credit back is the usual choice). To keep the classes (for example a special event on a holiday), untick **Cancel the classes already on these days**.
- **Recurring classes skip closed days** from then on. A course with a set number of classes still gets all of them: the missed class is added at the end.
- **On the schedule,** closed days are shaded and named, for staff and clients.
- **Adding or moving a class onto a closed day** asks you first, in case it's a mistake. You can still go ahead.
- **Edit** a closure to change its name or message. To change the days or location, remove it and add it again.
- **Remove a closure:** recurring classes it left out come back on the schedule. Classes it cancelled stay cancelled, because people were already told and given their class back. Add them again from the calendar if you need to.
- **Open on a public holiday?** There's nothing to do: classes run as normal unless you add a closure.

## Booking clients in

Owners and admins book clients from the class: tap the class, then **Book a client**.

Common reasons a booking can't be made:

- **The class is full.** Add them to the waitlist instead.
- **They're already booked** into that class.
- **No package covers the class.** The package may have run out, expired, or not cover that class type. Sell them a package first, or (if the studio allows it under Studio rules, "Allow bookings without a package") book them as "Pay at studio". You can also book it as complimentary.
- **Appointments** always need a package that covers appointments.
- **They haven't signed the waiver.** Studio members sign in the app at their first booking. Front of House or an admin can mark a paper waiver as done.
- **Weekly limit:** the studio limits classes per week, and they've reached it. Admins can go over it after a warning.
- **The booking window:** clients can only book between the window opening (e.g. 14 days before) and closing (e.g. 1 hour before). Admins can still book people in outside it, after a warning.
- **The class has finished or was cancelled** (for example by a closure).
- **ClassPass:** the class doesn't take ClassPass, or all its ClassPass spots are taken.
- **Your role:** only owners and admins can book clients in.

## Cancelling, moving and refunds

- **Cancel a booking:** tap the class, find the client in the class list, then **Cancel**. Outside the late-cancellation window, the class goes back to their package. Inside it, it counts as late: the class isn't given back and a late fee may apply (owners and admins can change both).
- **Move a booking:** the client's booking, then **Move**. It keeps the same package.
- **Refunds** are done by owners and admins only. For a class, use **Refund** on the booking in the class list (gives the class back). For money (a package, gift card, shop order or fee), go to **Sales**, open the sale and choose **Refund**: choose the amount, the reason, and whether it goes back the way they paid or as studio credit. You can also cancel what was bought (for example the package) at the same time. Card refunds go through your card terminal or payment provider; the app records them and emails the client.
- **Fees** (late cancellation, no-show) are listed under **Fees**, where admins collect or waive them.
- **When fees are final:** a late-cancellation fee is added when someone cancels inside the late-cancellation window, and it's final straight away. A no-show fee is added when someone is marked as a no-show, and it follows attendance until attendance **locks at midnight at the end of the class's day** (in the location's time zone). If attendance is corrected before then, the fee is removed. Until then the Fees page shows it as **Final after midnight**, and collecting it asks you first.
- **Attendance lock:** attendance opens an hour before class and locks at midnight at the end of that day. After that, instructors and Front of House can't change it. Owners and admins can still correct it, after a warning, because that adds or removes a no-show fee. If the client already paid the fee, refund it under **Sales**.

## Sales: invoices, receipts and refunds

Every sale gets an invoice (packages, membership renewals, gift cards, shop orders and fees). Once it's paid, it gets a receipt too. Owners and admins find them all under **Sales**:

- **Transactions:** what's been taken in, refunded and is still to collect, with search and filters. Open a sale to view or print its invoice or receipt, **Email receipt**, **Mark as paid** (choose how: card, cash, QR payment such as PayNow, bank transfer) or **Refund**.
- **Invoice design:** your business name, address, registration number (e.g. UEN), contact details, logo, accent colour, invoice and receipt numbering, sales tax (e.g. GST, shown as "includes"), how to pay, a note at the bottom, and whether receipts are emailed automatically. The preview updates as you type. Invoices already issued keep the details they were issued with.

Clients can see and print their own receipts under **My packages**, **Receipts and invoices**.

## Membership payments that fail

When a membership's renewal payment doesn't go through, the membership is **on hold**: the client can't book with it until it's paid. The card is tried again 3 and 7 days later, and the client is asked to update their card (they can pay straight away in the app). Owners and admins can also take the payment at the desk. If it still hasn't been paid after 7 days, the membership ends: upcoming bookings made with it are cancelled, any early termination fee applies (if it's within the minimum term), and to carry on the client buys a new membership, starting a new minimum term.

## Shop

Owners and admins add studio merch under **Shop**: name, price, photo, sizes or colours and how many you have. Gift card settings are here too. Clients see **Shop** in their menu once something's on sale (or gift cards are on); it disappears when the shop is empty. They order in the app and collect at the front desk, paying then (or with studio credit). Front of House hands orders over under **Pickups**, taking payment first if it isn't paid.

## Waitlists

Clients join a waitlist when a class is full. With **Book me in automatically**, the first person waiting gets the spot; with **Ask me first**, the spot is held for them for a set time while they confirm. Waitlists close when booking closes.

- **How waitlists work** (Studio rules, Waitlist): **Clients choose** lets each client pick one of the two when they join. **Always book in automatically** or **Always ask first** uses one way for everyone, and clients aren't asked. People already waiting keep the choice they made. When staff add someone to a waitlist, the same setting applies.

## Packages

Package types are set up under **Packages**: class packs or unlimited memberships, price, how long they last, which classes they cover, sharing and weekly limits. Selling one copies the terms, so editing a package type never changes packages already sold. Each booking uses the package that runs out soonest.

- **Extension requests** show at the top of **Packages**. If the client attached something (for example a doctor's note), tap it to open a **quick view** right there: PDFs show page by page, and photos as they are. Zoom with **+** and **−**, go back with **Fit width**, and close with **✕** or Esc. Nothing opens in a new window. **Download** saves a copy if you need one.

## Cover

Instructors ask for cover from their class ("Ask for cover"). The request goes to the class's teaching list and admins are told. Admins choose who's asked, approve offers or assign someone directly under **Cover requests**.

## Clients and leads

- **Clients:** everyone who books with you, with history, tags, packages and fees. Owners and admins only.
- **Leads:** people who aren't paying members yet (ad enquiries, walk-ins, ClassPass clients). When a ClassPass client buys one of your packages, they become a member.

## Communication

Read and reply to WhatsApp messages and emails from clients in one inbox. Filter by **All**, **WhatsApp** or **Email**. To bring in email, an owner or admin connects the studio's **Gmail** (or Google Workspace) or **Outlook** (or Microsoft 365) account under Communication, Settings, by signing in with Google or Microsoft. Replies go from that address, in the same email thread. Other email providers aren't supported yet.

WhatsApp works alongside the WhatsApp Business app on your phone. Meta charges for some messages (marketing templates always; service updates outside 24 hours of the client's last message). Before using it, an owner or admin chooses how messages are paid for under Communication, Settings: pay Meta directly, or buy message credits. Replies within 24 hours of a client's message are free.

## Reviews

After a class they came to, clients are asked how it went: 1 to 5 stars and an optional comment. Only their most recent class is ever asked about, so nobody is asked after every booking.

- **Skipping is fine.** If they skip, they're asked again after their next class.
- **"Don't ask me again for…"** Clients who leave a review can pause the pop-ups for 2, 4, 8 or 12 weeks (they can also do it later under **Feedback**, **My reviews**, once they've left one review).
- **Google reviews:** add your studio's Google review link under **Studio rules**, **Reviews and celebrations** (in your Google Business Profile, choose "Ask for reviews" and copy the link). After any review, the client is offered **Share on Google**, with their words copied so they can paste them. **Everyone is offered it, whatever they gave.** Offering it only to happy clients ("review gating") is against Google's rules and can get a studio's Google reviews removed. The Reviews page shows how many people tap it.
- **The Reviews page** (owners and admins, under Growth) shows the average, how people rated (5 to 1), the Google share rate, each instructor's average, and every review. Filter by period, class, instructor, location and stars; tap an instructor to see just theirs.
- **Instructors** see **My reviews**: reviews of their own classes, with clients' first names and initials only.
- **Low reviews** (2 stars or fewer) tell owners and admins straight away, and wait under **Feedback**, **Needs attention** until someone follows them up.

## Feedback

Clients can tell the studio what they'd like to see under **Feedback** in their menu: they choose what it's about (Facilities and comfort, Classes and schedule, Instructors, App and booking, Prices and packages, or Something else), optionally a location, and write a short message. For example: "The aircon at Tanjong Pagar is really cold."

- **Owners, admins and Front of House** see it under **Feedback**. Each topic has its own tab, so facilities issues don't get mixed up with schedule requests.
- **Needs attention** (the first tab) lists new feedback nobody has answered yet, and low reviews to follow up.
- **Reply** from the item: the client gets it by email and sees it in the app, and can reply back. Mark items **Replied** or **Sorted** as you go. If a client writes again about something marked Sorted, it goes back to New.
- **Follow up a low review** with **Mark as followed up**, adding a private reply if you like (the client gets it by email).

## Celebrations

Owners, admins and instructors get a pop-up on **Home** when there's something to celebrate:

- **Every two weeks, a recap.** Instructors: classes taught, people who came (and how many came back), how full their classes were, their busiest class and their reviews. Owners and admins: the same for the whole studio.
- **Milestones:** 50, 100, 250, 500 and more classes taught; for the studio, classes held and total visits.
- **With AI recaps on** (Studio rules), Claude writes the headline, a kind summary of what clients said in their reviews, and two self-care tips. It sees numbers and review text only, never names or contact details, and uses 1 AI credit per recap. With it off, the same card uses set wording.
- Each person can turn the pop-ups off for themselves with **Don't show these**, and back on under **Preferences**. Owners and admins can turn them off for everyone under Studio rules.

## Reports

**Reports** has tabs: Overview, Sales, Classes, Clients, Conversion and Instructors. Each number is compared with the period before. On the **Classes** tab, **Zoom in** narrows everything to a class type, location, instructor, days of the week or time of day (mornings, afternoons, evenings), and shows how full each location and time slot was.

**Export** (top right) gives you either **Raw data (CSV)**, every row behind the numbers for a spreadsheet or your accountant, or a **Visual report**, a one-page summary with the key numbers and charts to print or save as a PDF. Both follow the tab, period and zoom you're looking at. Client exports never include contact details.

## Agents

Agents are helpers that send reminders to staff (never clients) as push notifications to the staff app, and to the bell in the app. Owners and admins set them up under **Agents**:

1. Pick a ready-made agent (for example "Remind instructors about cover classes" or "Warn about quiet classes the night before").
2. Give it a name.
3. Adjust when it runs, what it checks and who it tells.
4. Choose what it can see: class times, who teaches, booking numbers (never names), and cover.

Agents never see client names, contact details, health notes, payments, prices, packages or pay, and they never change anything. They tell a person, who decides. Instructors only hear about their own classes. Anyone can mute an agent for themselves (Agents in the bell menu). **Pause all** stops every agent at once. **Preview** shows exactly what an agent would send right now, without sending it.

## Writers

Two writing agents save time on words:

- **Announcement writer:** on **Announcements**, use **Write it for me**. Describe it in a sentence ("lift broken at Tanjong Pagar, use the stairs") and it fills in the form for you to check. 1 AI credit.
- **Campaign drafter:** on **Marketing**, **Campaigns**, choose **Draft with AI**. It writes a marketing email in your studio's look and makes a draft campaign. Open the email to check it, then **Send…**. 3 AI credits.

They write in your studio's voice, only use facts you give them (anything missing becomes a [placeholder] to fill in), and never post or send anything themselves. They see class and location names, never client details.

## The assistant

The assistant (the **Ask** button) answers questions about using the app and about your studio: for example, why a client can't be booked into a class. It only sees what your role can see. It can offer to book a client in, add them to a waitlist or cancel a booking that's free to cancel, and does it only once you press **Confirm**.

It can't do anything involving money (refunds, payments, prices, selling packages, fees, pay), change settings, staff or the schedule, delete anything, or share client contact details or health notes. For those, it tells you who can and where.

Owners and admins can switch it off, choose which roles can use it, and stop it doing things for people, under **Agents**, then **Assistant**. Each plan includes a number of AI credits a month, shared by the assistant (1 per answer), the writers and AI recaps (1 per recap) (see **Agents**, then **Usage**). Reminder agents are free. Owners and admins are told at 80% and when they're used up, and can buy a top-up.

## Class types

**Class types** lists your group **Classes** and private **Appointments** separately, each with its own **+ New** button. Appointments show which packages cover them.

## Studio rules

Under **Studio rules**: the booking window, late-cancellation window and fees, no-show fees, weekly limits, whether clients need a package, whether staff can book without one, waivers, spot booking, waitlist holds and how cover works.

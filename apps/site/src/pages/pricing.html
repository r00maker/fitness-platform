    Questions studios ask

    Is there a contract?

    No. Plans are month to month and you can stop at the end of any month. Paying yearly is optional: it gets you two months free, and runs to the end of the year you paid for.

    What does moving my data cost?

    Nothing for a standard move: your clients, packages with the classes they have left, memberships and upcoming bookings, from one old system. If your move is unusual (several old systems, or custom data), we tell you the price before we start.

    Do you take a cut of payments?

    No. Clients pay into your studio's own Stripe account, and Stripe charges its standard fee, the same as using it directly. You can pass card and GrabPay fees on to clients if you like.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes, at any time. Moving up switches the new features on straight away. Before you move down, we show you exactly what would switch off, so nothing disappears by surprise.

    I have one location but want my own app.

    The app comes with Growth (SGD 99 a month more) and is included in Signature. For one location, Growth with the app is the cheapest way to get it, at SGD 498 a month.

    Do WhatsApp messages cost extra?

    Replying to clients who message you is free. Some messages your studio starts, like reminders, are charged by Meta per message. We pass that on at cost, with nothing added.

A day at the studio, handled.

Scroll through an ordinary day. The admin happens as you go, not at the end of it.

  1. An instructor asks for cover in the app and the ones who are free say yes. You approve with one tap, and the class, the instructors and everyone booked are told. No group-chat chasing.

  2. Someone cancels, and the next person on the waitlist is booked in and told. A class still under its minimum a few hours before is called off for you, with credits back and everyone told. No juggling.

  3. WhatsApp and email land in one shared inbox on the studio's own number. Whoever's on shift can answer without logging into your phone or your email, with the client's packs and bookings beside the chat.

  4. However you pay your instructors (per class, per head after five, by class size, hourly or salaried), each class adds to their pay the moment it finishes. Watch the month build day by day; at month end there's nothing to calculate, only to check, mark paid and export.

Switching is our job, not yours.

We move your clients, the classes they have left, memberships and upcoming bookings, and check every row with you before anything goes live. Most studios are moved within a day of sending their files.

How a move works

  1. Send us your exports

    From Mindbody, Momence, Glofox, TeamUp or a spreadsheet. Or add our team to your old system as a staff user and we'll take it from there.

  2. We check every row

    Dates, phone numbers and packs are read carefully, with a report of anything odd before a single client is moved.

  3. You compare a sample

    Ten of your clients, side by side with your old system, so you can see it's right with your own eyes.

  4. Your studio opens

    Clients sign in and book with the classes they had. Your old system can go the same week.

Nothing is guessed. Anything unclear comes back to you.

Before the move, the check lists what it couldn't be sure about, in plain words, with the row it came from. You answer; we move.

Every row ends up created, linked to an existing account, merged as a duplicate, or not moved with a reason. The four always add up, or the move doesn't go ahead.

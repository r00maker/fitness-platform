Anyone on your team can run the schedule

Every change is checked against your rules, your instructors and your rooms. Clashes, closed days and late cancellations are explained in plain English, with what happens next, before anything is saved.

New staff can't quietly break things, and you don't have to be the only one who knows how it all works.

Classes look after themselves

Someone cancels, and the next person on the waitlist is booked in and told. A class still under its minimum a few hours before is called off for you, with credits back and everyone told. No juggling.

Set it once per class: how many spots, whether a waitlist runs, and how many bookings a class needs to go ahead.

Pay, worked out as you go

However you pay your instructors (per class, per head after five, by class size, hourly or salaried), each class adds to their pay the moment it finishes. Watch the month build day by day; at month end there's nothing to calculate, only to check, mark paid and export.

Instructors see their own pay in the app, so the month-end questions stop.
